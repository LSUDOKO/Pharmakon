Dioscorides described about 600 medicinal plants.
Which ones did he mean — and was he right?

Names don't map to species

An ancient name can point at several modern plants, and translations hide that doubt. One wrong identification breaks every lead built on it.

Survival is studied by hand

Whether a remedy persisted from Dioscorides through Byzantium to today's villages has only been traced plant by plant.

Predictions are rarely tested

Herb–disease models are almost never checked with a time split, so "history predicts science" is assumed, not measured.

Method II — trace each use, then test with a time machine

Survival across 2,000 years

Dioscorides→Galen→John the Physician→modern Greece

For each name × body system: is the use attested in each era? Compared with a label-shuffle null (1,000 permutations), so chance overlap is not mistaken for tradition. Galen is checked for copying (word 4-gram containment).

Rolling-origin backtest

Stand at 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015. Rank plant–condition pairs nobody had studied, using only what was known then. Check PubMed up to 2025.

  • features dated ≤ cutoff (compound, ChEMBL and study years)
  • research-popularity baseline to beat
  • no LLM output, no LLM-derived claims, frozen non-LLM identification
  • cluster-bootstrap 95% intervals at every cutoff

Limits, ethics and what comes next

What we don't claim

  • Expert gold is judgement, not ground truth (61 names, wide intervals).
  • "Supported" = PubMed co-mention proxy; human audits generated, not yet filled.
  • Calibration 0.14 still misses ≤ 0.10.
  • LLM outputs are verified word-for-word but not yet expert-audited.

Responsible research

  • Research hypotheses only — no dosing, no medical advice.
  • Toxic genera flagged with PubChem citations.
  • No LLM output ever feeds the backtest (tested).
  • Nagoya Protocol: benefit-sharing for any commercial follow-up.

Next

  • Expert audit of claims, conditions and labels.
  • Richer floras (Flora of Greece, POWO with permission).
  • Paul of Aegina, Oribasius; Arabic and Latin transmission.
  • Screening partnership for top non-toxic leads; Zenodo DOI.
